# Mandatory input recovery

When required keyboard, Escape, mouse, gesture or scroll monitoring cannot be installed, stop the startup continuation and invalidate all installed input ports and remove the local keyboard monitor and registered Carbon shortcuts. Do not summon the switcher while recovering. Losing Accessibility after startup uses the same budget. Updates, user-requested restarts and other deliberate relaunches do not consume it.

An episode permits three attempts total: after the first failure, restart after one second; after the second, after three seconds; after the third, keep the process alive with a nonmodal recovery window. The only actions are Try again and Quit AltTab. Try again explicitly resets the budget and starts a new process; it never resumes a partially initialized process. Quit exits without another scheduled restart. A repeated failure notification in one process neither consumes another attempt nor queues another restart.

Persist the failure before scheduling a restart. Share the budget across failing controls and processes, serialize read/modify/write with a separate file lock, and replace one complete record atomically. A missing record starts a fresh episode. A corrupt or unwritable record stops automatic recovery immediately rather than granting unlimited retries. Manual retry must successfully reset the record before relaunching.

Only a completed startup followed by 60 seconds without entering recovery clears the budget. Use monotonic process uptime for this interval. A stable older instance cannot erase a newer failure: clearing compares the record generation captured at successful startup. Closing or crashing early retains the budget; a manual launch alone does not reset it.

DEBUG lifecycle QA uses the session's isolated Application Support directory. It can fail individual real input-installation sites until a configured number of failures, and inspect the persisted count. It uses production delays, real process relaunches and the real recovery window. Cleanup removes that session's recovery directory. No license endpoint, signature identity or production license storage changes are part of this feature.

Recovery after a completed startup cancels pending Pro history/anchor presentations and deferred offers before dismissing the switcher, and closes visible Pro offers. All new Pro offers, including user-triggered blocked-feature explanations, remain suppressed until a fresh process has recovered. License expiry still runs; recovery neither consumes an unseen offer nor resets the trial, opt-out or message budget. A cancelled Day 4 tour retains its existing Day 4/5 retry opportunity; an already seen expiry explanation is not replayed. ProPromptFlowTests covers dispatch suppression, pending/visible dismissal and late history; Golden Gate LIVE-RECOVERY-12/13 covers permission-loss integration and recovery with real windows and keyboard input.
