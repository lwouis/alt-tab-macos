# Speech bubbles

Discovery tips, feedback, trial announcements and Settings use `SpeechBubbleView` for their outline and content geometry. All use an 18-point content inset from the body on all four sides, a 4-point line gap and circular corners with a 24-point radius, reduced only for short bubbles. The tail and outline thickness are accounted for separately by the shared inset calculation. No consumer supplies its own padding or corner radius. Tips retain the switcher's material; other bubbles retain their native control background.

Tip titles use their natural single-line height instead of a padded fixed-height row. The title and instructions each occupy exactly one line. The 24-point close hit target sits within the shared content area beside the title and never overlaps the instructions. Width hugs both lines up to the existing cap and screen limit; unusually long text truncates visually while retaining the full accessibility wording. Tips never accept keyboard focus. Only the cross is visible at rest; its circular background appears on hover and clears when the panel closes. Search reads “Press [key]”. The extra-shortcut instruction reads “Keep holding [modifiers] then press [key]”. Neither instruction has a comma or trailing period. Both held modifiers and cycling keys are keycaps, with readable accessibility names and reordered placeholders supported. Dismissal semantics are unchanged. Trial/Settings and feedback mascots share a subtle silhouette shadow; discovery tips do not add this shadow.

Apple's WWDC26 AppKit guidance recommends relating corner shapes to their containers. The 24-point radius is an AltTab design choice for these separate speech bubbles, not an Apple-mandated value or a use of macOS 27's container-concentric API.
