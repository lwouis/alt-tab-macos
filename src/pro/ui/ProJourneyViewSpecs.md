# Speech bubbles

Discovery tips and feedback share one closed bubble outline, including the tail. The body uses circular corner arcs with a 24-point radius, reduced for short bubbles and when the switcher's supplied maximum is smaller. The tip retains the switcher's material; feedback retains its native control background. Tips inset text by 12 points from the body, separate the two lines by 4 points, and place a 24-point close hit target 8 points from the top/trailing edges, giving the cross itself about a 16-point optical inset. The title and instructions each occupy exactly one line; the standard tip is 76 points tall. Trial/Settings and feedback bubbles use 8-point vertical and 12-point horizontal content insets with a 4-point line gap. Width hugs both lines up to the existing cap and screen limit; unusually long text truncates visually while retaining the full accessibility wording. Its hit target stays clear of the instructions and it never accepts keyboard focus. Only the cross is visible at rest; its circular background appears on hover and clears when the panel closes. Search reads “Press [key]”. The extra-shortcut instruction reads “Keep holding [modifiers] then press [key]”. Neither instruction has a comma or trailing period. The extra-shortcut instruction renders both the held modifiers and the cycling key as keycaps, with readable names for accessibility and reordered placeholders supported. Dismissal semantics are unchanged. Trial/Settings and feedback mascots share a subtle silhouette shadow; discovery-tip mascots are unchanged.

Apple's WWDC26 AppKit guidance recommends relating corner shapes to their containers. The 24-point radius is an AltTab design choice for these separate speech bubbles, not an Apple-mandated value or a use of macOS 27's container-concentric API.
